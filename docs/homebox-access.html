C-Crate network access

On a laptop, phone, or tablet connected to the same network, open http://10.249.140.95:17745 and sign in with your C-Crate email and password. No SSH tunnel or launcher is needed.

For the direct laptop cable, open http://10.77.0.1:17745. On the Pi itself, use http://127.0.0.1:17745.

After changing Wi-Fi, open Connection Info on the Pi desktop to find its current address. Guest Wi-Fi can block access between devices. Internet is not required for C-Crate.

In C-Crate, open Collection → Members to issue, revoke and print personal QR badges. Choose Issue badge, confirm, then Print one badge before closing the dialog. Managers can choose Allow management for another collection member.

Each item’s details include borrowing status and a Loans & return notes tab. The existing label dialog also offers Print one label on Pi. The Workshop sidebar contains loans, storage, imports, labels, recovery and system status; it follows your C-Crate theme and collection. Equipment Terminal opens the terminal on port 17749 and links back to C-Crate. Both interfaces share the same records and Pi printer.

C-Crate listens on the Pi network interfaces and retains its normal login. Access from outside this network requires a separately configured remote access method.
